If a proposed use is intended for commercial advantage or monetary compensation, do not assume the noncommercial grant covers it. Obtain a separate written commercial license from the rights holder before beginning that use.

Examples that may require commercial permission include using Shadi as part of a paid data-preparation or data-extraction service, embedding it in a product sold to customers, deploying it as part of a revenue-generating operation, or redistributing it as part of a commercial offering. These are examples, not a substitute for the license's terms.

Contact the project owner through the [kzlca GitHub profile](https://github.com/kzlca). A public issue or this explanatory page is not itself a commercial license.

No commercial fee or pricing schedule is implied by the source-available license. Commercial terms must be agreed directly in writing.
