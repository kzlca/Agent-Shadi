Product focus
- Local-first data-preparation workbench for turning unstructured source material into structured, reviewable datasets.
- Multiple input methods: pasted text, supported files, built-in browser collection workflows, and customer-configurable web connectors.
- Workflow-specific schemas, examples, prompts, deterministic rules, provenance, normalization, quality checks, duplicate review, and human approval.
- Curated Golden Data is produced through controlled extraction, validation, human review, and export; AI output alone is not treated as ground truth.
- One manually selected page or channel per browser collection run.

Version
